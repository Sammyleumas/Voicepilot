## What VoicePilot does with your data

- **Voice.** When you tap the microphone (or turn on hands-free mode), audio is processed by your device's speech recognition service (for example, Google's). VoicePilot receives only the recognised text and does not store audio.
- **Commands.** Command text is processed on your device. If you turn on an AI provider, the text of the command and up to four recent conversation turns are sent to that provider (Google Gemini directly, or the VoicePilot server which forwards it to Google). Nothing else is sent.
- **Contacts.** Read on your device to find the person you asked to message or call. Never uploaded.
- **Notifications.** Only if you grant notification access and choose apps. Kept in memory on your device; never uploaded or stored.
- **History, notes, tasks, reminders, memory and routines.** Stored only on your device. You can turn history and memory off, set how long history is kept, and delete everything in Settings › Privacy.
- **API keys and tokens** you enter are encrypted on your device.
- **No advertising, no sale of data, no tracking SDKs.** Device backups exclude VoicePilot data.

## Third parties

Google (speech recognition on your device; Gemini if you enable AI). WhatsApp and other apps receive only what you send them yourself after VoicePilot opens them.

## Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data in the app at any time. For data held by the VoicePilot server (request counts only), contact [email].
